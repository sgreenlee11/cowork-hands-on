Session 01

Intro

Four starting points. Choose what fits your day.

01 / Calendar02 / Project03 / Meeting04 / Competitor

Run these prompts in Cowork, not on this website. WorkIQ uses the work context you are permitted to access; available sources and capabilities depend on your setup. If something is unavailable, ask Cowork to flag the gap rather than guess.

Option 01Plan + draft messages

Calendar health optimizer

Take a closer look at the next two weeks.

Review my calendar for the next two weeks. Identify meetings I can likely decline - no agenda, too many attendees, low relevance to my role. Protect my existing focus blocks. Flag conflicts. Give me an action plan with draft decline messages for the low-priority meetings. Keep this as a plan and drafts for my review. Do not make calendar changes or send messages without my explicit approval.
Option 02Word + Excel

Cross-channel project consolidation

Bring the project conversation into one place.

Search all my emails, Teams chats, and SharePoint files related to [project name] from the last month. Create a Word document organizing everything: key decisions made, open questions, responsible parties, timeline, and risks. Prepare a draft for my review. Flag missing information rather than inventing details.

Then, in the same conversation

Now turn the open questions and risks into a tracked Excel action list with owners, due dates, and status. Prepare a draft for my review. Mark unconfirmed owners or dates as to be confirmed.
Option 03Email draft + Excel

Meeting follow-up + action tracker

Turn a specific meeting into clear next steps.

Summarize my meeting [meeting name or link]. Extract all action items with owners and deadlines. Draft a follow-up email to all attendees with the summary and next steps. Also create an Excel tracker for the action items with columns for owner, task, deadline, and status. Keep the email as a draft for my review and approval; do not send it. Mark any unconfirmed owners or deadlines as to be confirmed.
Option 04Sourced PowerPoint brief

Sourced competitor briefing

Connect external research with internal context.

Deep research our competitor [competitor name]'s recent moves, announcements, and market positioning. Search my email and Teams for any internal discussions mentioning them. Create a PowerPoint competitive brief with: market overview, competitor moves, our positioning gaps, and recommended response - cite all sources. Prepare a draft for my review. Distinguish public research from internal discussions, flag unavailable sources, and do not invent facts or citations. Keep internal information and the resulting brief private in my Microsoft 365 workspace; do not upload them to this website or share without my approval.
