Option 01Plan + draft messages

Calendar health optimizer

Now turn the open questions and risks into a tracked Excel action list with owners, due dates, and status. Prepare a draft for my review. Mark unconfirmed owners or dates as to be confirmed.
Option 03Email draft + Excel

Meeting follow-up + action tracker

Turn a specific meeting into clear next steps.

Summarize my meeting [meeting name or link]. Extract all action items with owners and deadlines. Draft a follow-up email to all attendees with the summary and next steps. Also create an Excel tracker for the action items with columns for owner, task, deadline, and status. Keep the email as a draft for my review and approval; do not send it. Mark any unconfirmed owners or deadlines as to be confirmed.
Option 04Sourced PowerPoint brief

Sourced competitor briefing

Connect external research with internal context.

Deep research our competitor [competitor name]'s recent moves, announcements, and market positioning. Search my email and Teams for any internal discussions mentioning them. Create a PowerPoint competitive brief with: market overview, competitor moves, our positioning gaps, and recommended response - cite all sources. Prepare a draft for my review. Distinguish public research from internal discussions, flag unavailable sources, and do not invent facts or citations. Keep internal information and the resulting brief private in my Microsoft 365 workspace; do not upload them to this website or share without my approval.

Part 02Start in Copilot Chat

Build a skill around a proven task

Find what works before making it reusable.

  1. Pick a repeatable outcome. Choose a bounded task you do regularly, with accessible inputs and a result you can judge.
  2. Experiment in Cowork. Try a real example privately. Refine the prompt, sources, output, and review checklist until you can describe what good looks like.
  3. Capture the process, not the data. Ask Cowork to turn the approved approach into a skill with placeholders and approval gates. Test it on a second example in a new conversation before relying on it.

Copy into Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat

Help me find one repeatable task from my work that is a good fit for a Cowork skill. Use my recent work context from the last two weeks, where available and permitted. Suggest three bounded tasks with recurring steps, accessible inputs, a clear output, and a way to judge quality. Cite the work signals behind each suggestion; flag missing access rather than guessing. If context is insufficient, ask about my role and recurring tasks. Recommend one and ask me to choose. After I choose, give me a concise, copy-ready prompt to paste into Cowork. Define the outcome, input placeholders, steps, output format, source citations, and a "what good looks like" checklist. Have Cowork test one example with me and refine the process. Only after I approve the approach, create a reusable skill with instructions and placeholders, not private examples. Include a second-example test and require my approval before sending, sharing, scheduling, or changing anything outside the skill.
